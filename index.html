Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ingredients, delivery, and philosophy.

Does Diet Soda contain caffeine? +
Both Diet Classic and Zero Lime are 100% caffeine-free! You can enjoy them morning, afternoon, or right before bed without disrupting your sleep rhythm.
How do you achieve sweetness with zero sugar? +
We exclusively blend pure organic Stevia leaf extract with monk fruit and prebiotic chicory root fiber. This creates a round, clean sweetness profile without the sharp chemical aftertaste of aspartame or sucralose.
Is Diet Soda keto, diabetic, and vegan friendly? +
Yes! With 0g net carbs, 0g sugar, and zero glycemic response, our sodas are certified keto, diabetic-safe, 100% plant-based vegan, and gluten-free.
Where do you ship and what is the delivery time? +
We ship nationwide in the US with 2-3 day carbon-neutral express delivery. Subscriptions renew monthly with free doorstep delivery and an automatic 20% discount.
